About this page

Wolves went extinct in Denmark around 1813 and returned naturally from Germany in 2012. Since then the population has grown steadily and is now established across Jutland, which has made wolves a recurring news topic — covering everything from population counts and livestock attacks to encounters with tourists and the government's management response. This page is a hand-curated set of links to recent coverage; it links out to the original publishers rather than reproducing their articles.
